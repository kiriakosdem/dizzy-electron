The Electric Field

How charges create a field

A positive source charge creates an electric field that points away from it. A negative source draws the field inward. For a point charge of magnitude |Q|, the field strength at distance r is E = k|Q|/r². Doubling the distance reduces the strength to one quarter.

With several sources, their field vectors add. Between two equal positive charges, the fields cancel at the midpoint even though each charge produces a field there.

What moves the test charge?

A test charge q feels the force F = qE. Its acceleration is F/m. The lab holds the source charges fixed and follows the test charge as its velocity changes.

The potential display shows electric potential energy per unit charge. Each source contributes V = kQ/r. These contributions add with their signs.
