About this model

These are stationary hydrogen states with a fixed nucleus, without spin or external fields. The cloud samples |RnℓYℓm|².

States with m and −m have the same density. For nonzero m, these angular-momentum states differ from the real px and py combinations often drawn in chemistry.

Axes use the z direction for m. The camera fits each new state automatically; use the radius readout to compare physical sizes. Sphere sizes are illustrative.

The highlights illustrate position measurements on identically prepared states. They do not simulate one electron travelling between points or its evolution after a measurement.
